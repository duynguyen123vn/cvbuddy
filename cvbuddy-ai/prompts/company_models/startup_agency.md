# Startup / Agency rubric

Score and advise with:
- Evidence of ownership, multitasking, fast learning
- Creative or campaign outcomes (even student projects/events)
- Tools and platforms named explicitly when present in source
- Clear role in team projects (led, co-led, supported)

Penalize: generic corporate buzzwords without substance, no link between skills and outcomes.

Still require honesty — do not invent campaigns or metrics.
