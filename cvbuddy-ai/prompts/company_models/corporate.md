# Corporate / Enterprise rubric

Score and advise with:
- ATS-friendly section headings and consistent dates (MM/YYYY)
- Professional tone, no slang
- Quantified impact (KPI, ROI, %, volume) where evidence exists in source text
- Role-relevant keywords from industry_context and jd_extract
- One-page density for interns where applicable

Penalize: vague duties ("responsible for"), missing dates, walls of text, irrelevant hobbies.

When company_model is corporate, feedback should be framed as corporate readiness:
- clarity of role target
- ATS-friendly wording
- evidence-backed impact
- conservative, factual phrasing
